Attributions follow the Golden Dawn system as used in Crowley's Thoth deck (Book T titles, decans, court zodiac spans). In the Thoth deck the Emperor takes Tzaddi and the Star takes Heh; the RWS/Golden Dawn order reverses them. Decan dates are approximate and shift by a day or so each year.
